How to play

Find every fuse on the floor. When the last one goes in, the lights come back and the front door unlocks, and she hears it. Get to the door.

  • WASDwalk
  • Mouselook around (click to lock the cursor, Esc frees it and pauses)
  • Shiftrun (she hears running)
  • Ehide in a wardrobe, and get out again
  • 1phone: text her (every 45 s)
  • Left sidedrag to walk
  • Right sidedrag to look around
  • RUNhold to sprint (she hears running)
  • HIDEget into a wardrobe when you're next to one
  • PHONEtext her (every 45 s)

Her song gets louder the closer she is. If you can see her blue eyes, she can see you. If she's chasing you, a text makes her stop and leave for 15 seconds. Don't let her see you climb into a wardrobe.

Headphones on. Silent mode off.

Playing together: stand next to a downed teammate and hold EREVIVE for 3 seconds. The arrow in the top right corner points to them.

Play together

Up to 4 players. One of you creates a lobby and shares its code; the others join with it.

Join with a code

Together it's a bit harder: she hears a little more, she's a little faster (you can still outrun her) and there's an extra fuse per extra player. If she catches someone, the others have 50 seconds to revive them.
