She was a porcelain doll once. Blue glass eyes. Someone left her out in the rain… and she grew.

How to play

Every floor has puzzle boxes on its walls, a different kind on each floor: a tilting labyrinth, a sliding portrait, pipes, a music box, her face in rings. Walk up and press USE. Solve them all and the front door unlocks, and she hears it. Get to the door. Mistakes can be loud, and she doesn't wait while you play.

  • WASDwalk
  • Mouselook around (click to lock the cursor, Esc frees it and pauses)
  • Shift / Ctrlrun (she hears running)
  • Ehide in a wardrobe, and get out again; use a puzzle box
  • 1phone: text her (every 45 s)
  • Spacein a wardrobe: hold your breath (not for too long)
  • QRplaying together: ping what you look at; hold for the dance wheel, point with the mouse, let go to dance (moving stops it)
  • F3show frames per second and ping
  • Change any key in Settings → Controls.
  • Left sidedrag to walk
  • Right sidedrag to look around
  • RUNhold to sprint (she hears running)
  • HIDEget into a wardrobe when you're next to one
  • PHONEtext her (it recharges: 35 s on Easy, 45 s on Medium, 60 s on Hard)
  • BREATHin a wardrobe: hold to hold your breath (not for too long)
  • 📍💬playing together: ping what you look at; the dance wheel (tap one; moving stops it)

Her song gets louder the closer she is. If you can see her blue eyes, she can see you. If she's chasing you, a text makes her stop and leave for 15 seconds. Don't let her see you climb into a wardrobe.

When she stops right outside your wardrobe, hold your breath: she listens. Run out of air and you gasp, and she hears that too. Sometimes her footsteps fade away... and she's still there.

Dark, cracked floorboards creak loudly when you step on them, and she hears it from rooms away. Step around them.

The top bar is your fear. The longer she's near, the more your hands shake, and you start hearing things.

Headphones on. Silent mode off.

Playing together: a downed teammate has 90 seconds. Stand next to them and hold EREVIVE for 3 seconds. The arrow in the top right corner points to them. Out of the game you watch the others, and can warn them where she is every 30 seconds.
